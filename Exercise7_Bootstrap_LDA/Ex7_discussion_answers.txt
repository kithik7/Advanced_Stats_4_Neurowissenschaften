Which p-value is most robust and informative?
The bootstrap p-value is the most robust because it makes no
assumption about the normality of the underlying distribution or the two
groups having any rship bw their variances, it 
directly resamples the actual data. 
The parametric tests (both univariate
and LDA) rely on the assumption that the sampling distribution of the mean
is well approximated by a t-distribution, which is okay to assume because we only have 
n=30 per group, 
But said assumption isn't fact checked from the data. 
However, in this particular dataset all three
methods work, since the separation between groups is adequately large
that none of the methods would actually miss it 
The bootstrap approach is the most fitting choice if the data was not separated well 
or stood against the normality assumption significantly more than this dataset.
